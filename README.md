 Marketing Campaign & Funnel Performance Analysis

## 1. Project Overview

This project analyzes marketing campaign performance to understand channel performance, conversion efficiency, customer acquisition costs, revenue generation, and marketing funnel drop-offs.

The project uses **Python and Pandas** for data cleaning, metric calculation, and exploratory analysis, and **Power BI** for interactive visualization and dashboard development.

## 2. Objectives

- Analyze marketing performance across different channels
- Compare revenue generated by marketing channels
- Analyze conversions across different channels
- Calculate and compare conversion rates
- Analyze cost per conversion
- Calculate Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)
- Understand the marketing funnel from impressions to conversions
- Identify differences in campaign performance
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard

6. Marketing Metrics

The following metrics were calculated during the analysis:

CTR
CTR = Clicks / Impressions

CTR measures the proportion of impressions that resulted in clicks.

Lead Conversion Rate
Lead Conversion Rate = Leads / Clicks

This measures the proportion of clicks that resulted in leads.

Conversion Rate
Conversion Rate = Conversions / Leads

This measures the proportion of leads that resulted in conversions.

Cost per Lead
Cost per Lead = Spend / Leads
Cost per Conversion
Cost per Conversion = Spend / Conversions
ROAS
ROAS = Revenue / Spend

ROAS measures the revenue generated for each unit of advertising spend.

11. Limitations
The dataset is historical and does not represent real-time campaign activity.
The analysis is descriptive and exploratory.
No predictive marketing model was developed.
The analysis identifies relationships and performance differences but does not establish causation.
Campaign performance may depend on factors not included in the dataset.
